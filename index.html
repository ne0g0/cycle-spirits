About Pecaño

Origin

Fifteen years among friends

Jeff came home from Italy with a nocino recipe and a habit of sipping slowly. Miah ran a Monday potluck for ten years. One made the drink; the other made the table.

The pecans come from friends’ trees, picked green in July and August, then rested six months. Rubycello is the brighter one, from Texas Ruby Red grapefruit. Small batches, a few times a year.

Both are made to be sipped.
